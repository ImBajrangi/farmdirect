# FarmDirect — Project Overview

## 1. Executive Summary
**FarmDirect** is an ethical, mobile-first, farmer-to-consumer hyperlocal marketplace engineered for India. It connects local farmers directly with households, restaurants, and grocery buyers without predatory middlemen.

## 2. Core Business Problem
In conventional Indian agricultural supply chains:
- Produce moves through 4 to 6 intermediary tiers (village aggregators, APMC mandi commission agents, wholesalers, logistics middlemen, retail cart vendors).
- Farmers receive only **20% to 35%** of the consumer rupee.
- Food reaches kitchens 48 to 72 hours post-harvest, deteriorating taste and nutritional integrity.
- Many unverified labels misuse "organic" claims without evidence.

## 3. The FarmDirect Solution
- **Direct Farmgate Realization**: 85% to 90% of order value flows straight into the farmer's bank or UPI account.
- **Freshness Window**: Produce is picked to order in the morning and delivered within 12 to 24 hours.
- **Pilot Geographic Service Area**: Maharashtra Peri-Urban Agricultural Corridor (Nashik: Dindori, Niphad; Western Pune: Haveli, Baramati).
- **Multi-Farmer Partitioning**: Customers can order items from multiple farms in a single cart; the system automatically partitions them into distinct sub-orders for each farm producer.
- **Honest Claims**: Zero false "organic" marketing. Produce is labeled "Lab Certified Organic" only when backed by valid NPOP or PGS-India lab documentation.
